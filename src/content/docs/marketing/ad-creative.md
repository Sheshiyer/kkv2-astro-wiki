### Creative Protocol

#### Steps

##### Item 1

- **Step Name:** Define Beta Audience
- **Step Description:** Separate premium seekers, practitioners, and investor/internal readers before writing copy.
###### Step Output

- **Target Audience:** premium seekers in transition; verified practitioners; founder and investor review layer
##### Item 2

- **Step Name:** Develop Source-Bound Creative
- **Step Description:** Use Mirror, tokens, consent handoff, Thailand/India, and reviewed visual assets. Do not use crowdfunding frames, fake proof, or placeholder media URLs.
###### Step Output

- **Ad Creative:** Mirror UI, token ledger, consent seal, practitioner trust map
##### Item 3

- **Step Name:** Optimize For Paid Activation
- **Step Description:** Measure whether users recharge after meaningful token use, not whether they only join a list.
###### Step Output

- **Primary Metric:** recharge of at least 299 within 90 days
### Comprehensive Narrative

- **Example:** Klear Karma is a Mirror-guided trust layer for seekers and verified practitioners. The pre-launch campaign should make one promise: reflect before you browse. The proof to seek is paid activation through recharge, repeat token use, and completed practitioner handoff, not vanity reach.
#### Alternatives

- A private beta for seekers who want context before choosing support.
- A practitioner trust layer designed to reduce generic leads and improve consent-safe handoff.

## Live Campaign Ads

### Campaign Ads

#### Headline Families

##### Item 1

- **Family:** Beta Access
###### Headlines

- Start with reflection before practitioner search
- Mirror helps you name what kind of support you need
- A quieter way to find verified spiritual support
###### Bodies

- Klear Karma begins with Mirror-led reflection, not category-first browsing. New users receive 369 free tokens and can use the opening flow before deciding whether to recharge.
- For seekers in Thailand and India beta circles: use Mirror to form a consent-safe seeker map before moving toward practitioner support.
##### Item 2

- **Family:** Token Utility
###### Headlines

- 369 free Mirror tokens to begin
- Use tokens after Mirror creates context
- Recharge only if reflection creates value
###### Bodies

- The opening Mirror/chart flow costs roughly 108 tokens. Paid activation begins when a user recharges at least 299 within 90 days of signup or interaction.
- Klear Karma is validating token utility before broad acquisition. The first proof point is recharge after meaningful Mirror use.
##### Item 3

- **Family:** Practitioner Trust
###### Headlines

- Context before handoff
- Verified practitioners need better context, not more generic leads
- Consent-safe seeker maps for high-context support
###### Bodies

- Mirror helps a seeker summarize what they are carrying, then shares only consent-approved context with verified practitioner support.
- The practitioner marketplace is the fulfillment layer. The moat is trust, context, and paid intent.
##### Item 4

- **Family:** Safety Boundary
###### Headlines

- Symbolic context, not certainty
- Mirror reflects patterns. It does not decide for you
- Spiritual support with consent boundaries
###### Bodies

- Klear Karma does not claim clinical authority, promised outcomes, prediction, or spiritual authority. Mirror helps structure reflection and next steps.
- Optional birth data is treated as symbolic context, never certainty.
