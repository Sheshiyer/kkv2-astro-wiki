#### Problem Agitation

- Most wellness marketplaces ask for category, price, and time before understanding the person.
- Practitioner quality is difficult to assess from generic profiles.
- Spiritual apps can overstate certainty or feel unserious.
- Seekers worry about privacy when sharing birth details or vulnerable context.
#### Failed Alternatives

- Generic wellness directories
- Unverified practitioner profiles
- Certainty-based spiritual apps
- Physical product or packaging-focused wellness brands
### New World

#### Mechanism Reveal

- Mirror creates reflection before marketplace browsing.
- Token recharge becomes the paid-activation signal.
- Consent-safe context handoff helps practitioners serve with more relevance.
- Profile-scoped commerce stays reviewed instead of becoming a generic shop.
#### Feature Stacking

- 369 free tokens to start
- 108-token opening flow
- Recharge anchors of 299, 599, 799, and 1099 in THB or INR
- Verified practitioner profiles
- Founder-reviewed marketplace listings in beta

- Mirror output includes seeker map summary, resonance threads, support modality weights, suggested next action, consent-safe healer preview, unlockable deeper sections, and fallback/manual-state data when AI is unavailable.
- Optional birth data is symbolic context, not certainty.
