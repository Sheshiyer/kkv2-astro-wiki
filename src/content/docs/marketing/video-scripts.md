# Video Scripts

## Campaign Video Script

**CTA:** Join the invite-led beta when Klear Karma opens access in Thailand and India.

**Total Duration:** 180 seconds

### Script Outline

**Hook:** Most wellness marketplaces ask you to pick a category before they understand what you are carrying.

**Problem:** For seekers, that can turn a vulnerable moment into browsing, filtering, and guessing. For practitioners, it creates generic leads instead of useful context.

**Hero:** Klear Karma starts with reflection before marketplace choice.

**Mechanism:** Mirror asks for lived context one answer at a time, summarizes what it heard, and asks for confirmation or refinement.

**Product Reveal:** That reflection becomes a consent-safe seeker map, tokenized intent, and a possible handoff to verified practitioner support.

**How It Works:** New users receive 369 free Mirror tokens. The opening Mirror/chart flow costs roughly 108 tokens. If the experience is useful, the user can recharge at local anchors of 299, 599, 799, or 1099 in THB or INR.

**Safety Proof:** Mirror uses symbolic context carefully. It does not claim clinical authority, promised outcomes, prediction, certainty, or spiritual authority.

### Feature Highlights

**Reflection Before Browsing:** The seeker names the question or transition first, then receives a structured map before choosing support.

**Consent-Safe Handoff:** Practitioners receive only the context the seeker agrees to share.

**Tokenized Paid Intent:** Recharge after meaningful token use becomes the first business KPI.

**Proof Status:** Public customer quotes and performance claims are intentionally omitted until beta data can be cited.

**Offer:** Invite-led beta access for seekers and practitioners in the first Thailand and India launch circles.

**Urgency:** The beta stays narrow so the team can validate recharge, repeat token utility, and practitioner handoff quality before broad scaling.

**Narrative:** Klear Karma is built around a simple sequence: reflect, map context, spend tokens, and move toward trusted support when ready. The product should not promise transformation, certainty, or clinical outcomes. The public story should make the practical loop clear: 369 free tokens, roughly 108 tokens for the opening flow, local recharge anchors, verified practitioner handoff, and a founder-controlled beta designed to prove paid activation before broad launch.

### Alternative Script

**Hook:** Before you book a practitioner, Mirror helps you name what kind of support you are actually looking for.

**Problem:** Generic profiles rarely capture the context behind a seeker's question.

**Mechanism:** Klear Karma is a Mirror-guided trust layer for reflection, tokenized intent, and practitioner handoff.

**Product Reveal:** The marketplace is the fulfillment layer, not the core moat.

**How It Works:** Mirror asks, summarizes, confirms, and only then routes toward support.

### Edge Cases

**Clinical Support Needs:** Make clear that Klear Karma is not a clinical service and route away from spiritual guidance claims.

**Privacy Concerns:** Let the seeker continue with limited context or stop before practitioner handoff.
