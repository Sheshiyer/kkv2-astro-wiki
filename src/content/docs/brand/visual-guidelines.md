# Visual Guidelines

## Palette & Typography

- **Primary:** Mirror Black #090711
- **Secondary:** Karma Violet #221245
- **Accent:** Quiet Gold #D8B35A
- **Header font:** Sora
- **Body font:** Inter

## Visual Identity Summary

### Visual Identity Core

#### Visual Strategy

- **Mood:** Reflective & Premium
- **Description:** A dark, premium, app-native visual system built around Mirror fields, lotus geometry, token ledgers, and consent-safe practitioner context.
- **Rationale:** The reflective and premium mood aligns with the MDS and Persona, conveying a sense of calmness, trust, and high-end quality.
#### Color Psychology

##### Primary Color

- **Name:** Mirror Black
- **Hex:** #090711
- **Psychological Impact:** Seriousness, trust, and high-end quality.
##### Secondary Color

- **Name:** Karma Violet
- **Hex:** #221245
- **Psychological Impact:** Luxury, creativity, and wisdom.
##### Accent Color

- **Name:** Quiet Gold
- **Hex:** #D8B35A
- **Psychological Impact:** Value, sophistication, and warmth.
#### Typography

##### Header Font

- **Name:** Sora
###### Weights

- Regular
- SemiBold
- Bold
- **Display Weight:** SemiBold
- **Emphasis Weight:** Bold
- **Case:** title case
##### Body Font

- **Name:** Inter
###### Weights

- Regular
- Medium
- SemiBold
##### Accent Font

- **Name:** JetBrains Mono
###### Weights

- Regular
- Medium
#### Logo Guidelines

- **Placement:** Centered, with clear space around it.
- **Clear Space:** At least 1/4 of the logo's width.
- **Variations:** Primary logo, secondary logo, and icon-only logo.
#### Imagery Art Direction

- **Photography Style:** App-native spatial compositions, no stock faces, no generic healer portraits.
- **Illustration Style:** Lotus geometry, trust-ledger diagrams, consent-safe data constellations, and premium app interface panels.
#### Graphic Elements

- **Shapes:** Minimal lotus geometry, token nodes, and consent seals.
- **Textures:** Black mirrored glass, deep violet vapor gradient, and quiet gold inlay.
- **Patterns:** Lotus-line geometry, token ledger chips, and reflective orbital rings.

#### Edge Cases

- **Logo Usage:** Avoid using the logo on busy or dark backgrounds.
- **Color Usage:** Avoid using the primary color for text or backgrounds.
