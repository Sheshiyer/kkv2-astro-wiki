# Partner FAQ

## Why this company? Why now?

The wellness marketplace is at an inflection point. Post-pandemic demand for mental health, spiritual guidance, and alternative healing has exploded. But the infrastructure connecting seekers with practitioners has not evolved. Directories still function like Yellow Pages — optimized for discovery speed, not for trust depth.

Klear Karma enters at the moment when seekers are sophisticated enough to know they need context before they choose, but no platform is designed to give them that context. The timing is not about market size. It is about market readiness for a trust-first approach.

## How does this make money?

Three revenue streams:

1. **Token micro-purchases** — Seekers buy Mirror tokens to access the reflection engine. Non-refundable, immediate revenue.
2. **Practitioner subscriptions** — Premium tiers (1,200–3,600 THB/month) for enhanced profiles, analytics, and priority in the recommendation flow.
3. **Reviewed contextual commerce** — Profile-scoped product listings can carry revenue share only after trust and practitioner context are established.

Klear Karma does **not** take a consultation commission in the current MVP or early-growth phase.

Break-even is ~15 premium practitioners or 45 active seekers/month. Achievable within 90 days of launch.

## What stops someone from copying this?

The protocol is not the moat. The moat is the data generated by the protocol.

Every Mirror flow produces consented, anonymized pattern data about what seekers need and what practitioners offer. Over time, this corpus becomes a proprietary matching engine that no competitor can replicate without running the same protocol for the same duration with the same community.

Additionally, practitioner network effects compound: more quality practitioners attract more seekers, which attracts more practitioners. The trust layer becomes stickier as the network grows.
