## Product Details

**Name:** Klear Karma Mirror

**Starting Recharge Anchor:** 299

**Description:** A mobile app-native reflective guide that turns seeker context into a consent-safe seeker map, token spend, and verified practitioner handoff.

**Summary:** Mirror is the paid-intent engine for Klear Karma. It asks for lived context before category, date, time, or price, then helps route a seeker toward relevant support without claiming certainty or authority.

## Technical Specs

**Format:** Mobile app interface and reflective digital Mirror experience

**Visual Materials:** Black mirrored glass interface language, quiet gold token lines, translucent app panels, lotus-line geometry, and consent-seal overlays

**Token Capacity:** 369 free Mirror tokens for new users; roughly 108 tokens for the opening Mirror/chart flow; recharge anchors of 299, 599, 799, and 1099 in local THB or INR denominations

**Terms Status:** Public support, refund, and availability terms are not defined in the source pack and should be approved before publication.

## Core Features

### Mirror Token Flow

Turns reflection into measurable token intent. Gives the team a concrete paid-activation loop to validate instead of optimizing only for free signups.

### Practitioner Trust Layer

Uses verified practitioner profiles, tiered session offers, and consent-safe context handoff. Helps seekers move toward high-context support while protecting sensitive context boundaries.

### Protocol-Reviewed Commerce

Keeps product listings profile-scoped, token-staked, and manually reviewed in beta. Prevents the marketplace from becoming a generic product tab before trust and fulfillment are proven.

## How It Works

**Step 1:** A seeker enters the invite-led beta and receives 369 free Mirror tokens.

**Step 2:** Mirror asks one primary answer at a time, summarizes what it heard, and asks for confirmation or refinement.

**Step 3:** The opening flow uses roughly 108 tokens and creates a consent-safe seeker map.

**Step 4:** The seeker can recharge tokens and move toward a verified practitioner handoff when ready.

**Step 5:** Practitioner and product fulfillment remain reviewed and narrow during beta.

## Outcomes

**Emotional Outcome:** The seeker feels reflected before being sold to.

**Practical Value:** The team can measure Mirror-to-recharge conversion, token velocity, AI-to-human handoff rate, repeat token utility, practitioner earnings activation, trust liquidity, protocol leverage, and contextual commerce conversion.

## Competitive Position

### What We Are Not

**Generic wellness marketplaces:** They often begin with category, price, and availability before understanding the person.

**Certainty-based spiritual apps:** They can overstate authority or prediction instead of using symbolic context carefully.

## Edge Cases

**Clinical Support Needs:** Mirror should route away from spiritual guidance and make clear that Klear Karma is not a clinical service.

**Unverified Practitioners:** Keep the practitioner outside fulfillment until profile completion, KYC/payment verification, and beta review requirements are met.
