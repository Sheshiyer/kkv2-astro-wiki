# Product Specifications

## Canonical Product Spec

- **Name:** Klear Karma Mirror
- **Description:** An app-native reflective guide that turns seeker context into a consent-safe seeker map, token spend, and verified practitioner handoff.
- **Physical form:** Mobile app interface and reflective digital Mirror object only: no physical box, bottle, kit, packaging, candle, apparel, or catalog product.

## Materials

- black mirrored glass
- deep violet vapor gradient
- quiet gold inlay
- translucent app panels
- lotus-line geometry
- token ledger chips
- consent seal overlays
- reflective orbital rings
- soft volumetric light
- dark premium interface chrome

## Detailed Product Source

### Product

- **Name:** Klear Karma Mirror
- **Starting Recharge Anchor:** 299
- **Description:** A mobile app-native reflective guide that turns seeker context into a consent-safe seeker map, token spend, and verified practitioner handoff.
- **Summary:** Mirror is the paid-intent engine for Klear Karma. It asks for lived context before category, date, time, or price, then helps route a seeker toward relevant support without claiming certainty or authority.
### Specs

- **Format:** Mobile app interface and reflective digital Mirror experience
- **Visual Materials:** Black mirrored glass interface language, quiet gold token lines, translucent app panels, lotus-line geometry, and consent-seal overlays
- **Token Capacity:** 369 free Mirror tokens for new users; roughly 108 tokens for the opening Mirror/chart flow; recharge anchors of 299, 599, 799, and 1099 in local THB or INR denominations
- **Terms Status:** Public support, refund, and availability terms are not defined in the source pack and should be approved before publication.
### Features

#### Item 1

- **Name:** Mirror Token Flow
- **Function:** Turns reflection into measurable token intent.
- **Value:** Gives the team a concrete paid-activation loop to validate instead of optimizing only for free signups.
#### Item 2

- **Name:** Practitioner Trust Layer
- **Function:** Uses verified practitioner profiles, tiered session offers, and consent-safe context handoff.
- **Value:** Helps seekers move toward high-context support while protecting sensitive context boundaries.
#### Item 3

- **Name:** Protocol-Reviewed Commerce
- **Function:** Keeps product listings profile-scoped, token-staked, and manually reviewed in beta.
- **Value:** Prevents the marketplace from becoming a generic product tab before trust and fulfillment are proven.
### Usage

#### Item 1

- **Step:** 1
- **Description:** A seeker enters the invite-led beta and receives 369 free Mirror tokens.
#### Item 2

- **Step:** 2
- **Description:** Mirror asks one primary answer at a time, summarizes what it heard, and asks for confirmation or refinement.
#### Item 3

- **Step:** 3
- **Description:** The opening flow uses roughly 108 tokens and creates a consent-safe seeker map.
#### Item 4

- **Step:** 4
- **Description:** The seeker can recharge tokens and move toward a verified practitioner handoff when ready.
#### Item 5

- **Step:** 5
- **Description:** Practitioner and product fulfillment remain reviewed and narrow during beta.
### Summary

- **Emotional Outcome:** The seeker feels reflected before being sold to.
- **Practical Value:** The team can measure Mirror-to-recharge conversion, token velocity, AI-to-human handoff rate, repeat token utility, practitioner earnings activation, trust liquidity, protocol leverage, and contextual commerce conversion.
### Alternatives

#### Item 1

- **Name:** Generic wellness marketplaces
- **Drawbacks:** They often begin with category, price, and availability before understanding the person.
#### Item 2

- **Name:** Certainty-based spiritual apps
- **Drawbacks:** They can overstate authority or prediction instead of using symbolic context carefully.
### Edge Cases

#### Item 1

- **Scenario:** A seeker needs clinical, emergency, or crisis support.
- **Response:** Mirror should route away from spiritual guidance and make clear that Klear Karma is not a clinical service.
#### Item 2

- **Scenario:** A practitioner is not yet verified or payout-ready.
- **Response:** Keep the practitioner outside fulfillment until profile completion, KYC/payment verification, and beta review requirements are met.
